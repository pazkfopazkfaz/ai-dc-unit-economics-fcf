This document, From Unit Economics to Free Cash Flow: A Mathematical Model of Financing-Driven Expansion in AI Data Centers, organizes a supplied AI data-center financing model into a reproducible analytical framework. It starts from unit EBITDA, unit capital expenditure, and debt service, derives company-level free cash flow and the critical expansion speed, and then extends the analysis to utilization dependence, residual-value risk, endogenous credit spreads, construction delays, training/inference revenue-mix volatility, off-balance-sheet structures, and stochastic uncertainty. Under the stated baseline inputs—unit EBITDA of 500, unit capex of 3,950, debt of 2,500 per MW, and an 8.5% debt cost—the critical annual expansion rate is approximately 7.3% on a levered basis and 12.7% unlevered, compared with the manuscript’s cited industry expansion range of 50%–90%. The model also shows that DSCR remains below the 1.20 covenant threshold even at full utilization under the tested debt structures; low-state collateral coverage is about 0.08 without a guarantee and 0.18 with a guarantee; and the probability of quarterly DSCR falling below 1 is approximately 26%–32% under the stated normal approximation. The document is suitable as a diagnostic framework for analyzing capital-intensive expansion, debt sustainability, and refinancing risk in AI data centers. Numerical inputs and company-level calibration claims are retained from the source manuscript and have not been independently validated; the document is not investment advice or a precise default-probability estimator.
